# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Huikang Liu, Aras Selvi, and Wolfram Wiesemann for making a
careful study of Gaussian-mixture mechanisms and privacy-utility tradeoffs
available for independent scrutiny. The explicit mechanism definitions,
tables, theorem statements, and appendix claims made it possible to
reproduce the aggregate numbers, reconstruct the proof-level checks, and
test the privacy assumptions directly.

This repository is an independent reproduction and audit effort. The authors
are not responsible for its code, counterexamples, benchmark implementations,
verdict labels, interpretation, or limitations. Falsifying a reported
privacy-qualified claim is recorded as a scientific audit result, not as a
statement about the authors' intent.
